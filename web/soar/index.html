Soar

Volumetric cloud rendering, in a tab

Fly through a cloud field the way light does — marching through the volume, scattering many times before it reaches you. No installation, and the file never leaves your machine.

Checking what this browser can do…

What this is

cloudyview's volumetric renderer, running on your GPU through WebGPU. Single scattering plus a multiple-scattering approximation, a physically-based sky, and an ocean below. It is not a path tracer — it is fast enough to fly.

Controls

Click the view to take the mouse. WASD to move, Space and Shift for up and down, scroll to change speed, Esc for the menu. Everything else lives in the menu.
